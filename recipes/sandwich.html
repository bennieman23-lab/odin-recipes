Simple Sandwich

Description

This is the simplest recipe for a sandwich available. Quick and easy meal.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Butter
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Optional - Salt

Instructions

  1. Cut the bread into slices
  2. Spread butter on the slices
  3. Place slices of the tomato and cucumber on one of the slices
  4. Optional - Add salt to tast
  5. Place the other slice on top of the other
  6. Enjoy
